Never use the AskUserQuestion tool (option pickers). Ask questions as plain text in the reply, with the options written out as a short numbered list, and wait for the user's typed answer.

**Why:** The user said "Please ask questions without pickers in the future" after a picker appeared. They use NVDA ([[screen-reader-user]]), and plain-text questions are easier for them.
**How to apply:** When a decision is needed, end the reply with the question and numbered options (say which one you recommend), then stop and wait.

**One question at a time** (user, 2026-09-26: "Please ask one question at a time in the future."). Never put two or more questions in one reply; ask the most important one, wait for the answer, then ask the next.
